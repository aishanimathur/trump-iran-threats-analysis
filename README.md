# trump-iran-threats-analysis
Article Name: The Mother of All Bluffs?

An analysis of the reported possibility of the United States using the Mother of All Bombs (MOAB) against Iran's suspected underground nuclear facility at Pickaxe Mountain, examining Trump's past threats to assess the credibility of this latest possibility.

Research Question
- How credible is the prospect of a MOAB strike against Pickaxe Mountain, given Trump's previous pattern of threatening military action against Iran?
